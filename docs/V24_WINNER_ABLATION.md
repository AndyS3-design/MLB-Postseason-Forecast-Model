# V24 — Winner features: older-season selection, newer-season retrospective checks

## Why this experiment

The expanded logistic classifier selected 55.6% of 2025 regular season winners (2,425 games) and 53.2% of 2025 playoff winners (47 games); neither approaches 90%. Elo and the full 2026 postseason simulation have also fallen far short of the requested 27/30 threshold. Reducing features may improve generalization; this is a **test**, not a promised change.

## Fixed study design

1. Use only features generated by matchup_model.feature_frame **from game days strictly BEFORE each match**, and specifically avoid 2025/2026 starting-pitcher identity and same-day observed box scores.
2. Train logistic winner classifiers on 2023 regular-season outcomes, select among three predeclared feature groups and C in {0.003, 0.03, 0.3} based on **2024 regular-season Brier alone**. Select Elo-only when none beat Elo's validation Brier.
3. Refit the chosen classifier on 2023-2024 regular-season games and report winner accuracy, Brier and log loss against the SAME-game Elo baseline on 2025 regular/playoffs and 2026 regular/playoffs.
4. Never use 2025 or 2026 winners to choose features or C; no retrospective 90% claim. Even those cohorts were seen by earlier project development, so they are retrospective diagnostics rather than independent forward evaluations.
5. Keep production V23 model, its published winner and winner+Over markets, and every frozen original unchanged.

The research workflow is `.github/workflows/mlb-v24-winner-ablation.yml`. Detailed machine-readable outputs are GitHub Actions artifacts.

## Promotion and model accuracy

Requiring 90% in all playoff games is aspirational, not a calibration guarantee. If this study loses to Elo, reject its candidate. If it improves probability metrics, future genuinely timestamped matched pregame predictions are still required before deployment. Do not select on the sample being presented as a holdout, omit losses, or backfill old forecasts.
