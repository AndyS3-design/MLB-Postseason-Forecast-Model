MLB PREDICTION LAB - FINAL SCORE ADD-ON
October 12, 2026

This add-on is for the existing upgraded MLB Prediction Lab v2 project.

1. Stop Streamlit in Developer PowerShell with Ctrl+C.
2. Make a full BACKUP of your existing MLB_Backtest_Expanded_2026_Postseason_Markets folder.
3. Extract this ZIP. Copy the 5 Python files alongside your existing dashboard.py,
   replacing dashboard.py and run_all.py when asked. The three new Python files
   (score_projection.py, score_backtest.py, test_score_projection.py) are additive.
4. Optionally copy outputs/score_metrics_2025.json to your existing outputs folder.
   Or regenerate it in the next steps.
5. DO NOT replace your data/ folder or your 2026 importer/matchup_model.py.
6. In Visual Studio Developer PowerShell (inside the project folder):

   py -m unittest discover -q
   py score_backtest.py
   py run_all.py
   py -m streamlit run dashboard.py

7. Select a matchup. In Game forecasts expand:
    "Projected final score — experimental"
   For scores evaluation open "Backtest & data quality".

SCOPE AND IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- Elo remains the primary winner forecast.
- Experimental score probabilities come from historical run scoring (2023-24 fitted),
  with a within-total adjustment to align win chance to Elo.
- The score display uses a representative exact score consistent with the Elo pick;
  it is not a reliably predictable result.
- Tied regulation scores get estimated extra-inning runs. All extra runs are assigned
  to the extra-inning winner as a simplification; actual teams can both score.
- Score model is not calibrated for betting, does not use verified present-day
  lineups/weather, and is not a validated profitable trading model.
- The extended matchup model and detailed pitcher/bullpen importers are UNCHANGED.
  This score module does NOT claim to incorporate their pitcher matchup effects.
- The 2025 score test is NOT a fresh pristine evaluation: this season has been
  studied during development of earlier models.
- The real 2026 data is only available on the user's computer. Python tests and
  the 2025 historical holdout were run with the included 2023-25 logs.
